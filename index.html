01 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology that serves
the community.

BayanLedger empowers local public project monitoring by harmonizing physical construction progress, fund allotments, and milestone verifications into a tamper-proof municipal ledger.

OUR MISSION

Public projects.
Clearer records.

Designed hand-in-hand with the municipal planning realities of Sta. Cruz, Laguna, BayanLedger eliminates information silos and manual discrepancies between engineering, budgeting, and public reports.

04 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW

From challenges
to clearer processes.

This study investigates how decentralized ledger technology (DLT) can be applied to local government project monitoring to eliminate paper-based bottlenecks and foster participatory budget transparency.

RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Design, develop, test, and evaluate BayanLedger for the Municipal Planning and Development Office of Sta. Cruz, Laguna in accordance with ISO/IEC 25010 software quality standards.

PROBLEMS THE PROJECT ADDRESSES
01

Limited Real-Time Visibility: Delays between physical site progress and budget status updates.

02

Vulnerable Paper Trails: Manual filing systems susceptible to transcription errors or misplacement.

03

Disjointed Fund Tracking: Difficulty cross-referencing SARO allocations directly with physical accomplishment percentages.

04

Fragmented Audit Reports: Slower reconciliation cycles for municipal planners and government auditors.

Academic capstone research conducted for the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Laguna.
